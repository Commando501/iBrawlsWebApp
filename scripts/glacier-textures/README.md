# Glacier Hockey Rink surfaces and light

Run `node scripts/glacier-textures/author-surfaces.mjs` to reproduce the four shipped PNGs and their SHA-256 manifest. The dependency-free authoring script produces original periodic PBR maps without downloads or source imagery.

The 6 m opaque ice tile carries broad frost roughness, shallow granular relief and sub-millimetre skate wear. Color stays quiet: luminance standard deviation is 0.99/255 and the total range is under 6/255. Narrow skate traces primarily affect the normal map; there are no bright cracks, painted grids, baked shadows, loose debris, displacement, transmission or real-time reflection captures. The medium blue-gray floor retains player silhouette contrast. Ice cliffs share these texture sources with independent handles and a deeper blue material tint; their larger strata and snow caps come from authored geometry.

High and Balanced use 1024 px sRGB albedo, OpenGL (+Y) tangent normals and linear roughness. Low uses averaged 512 px albedo and scalar roughness. The four original maps total 1,433,228 bytes (approximately 1.37 MiB). The manifest records dimensions, physical scale, luminance statistics, sizes and hashes. Tests check periodic edges, shallow physical relief, file budgets and floor contrast.

Architecture reuses the established Stadium V3 ceramic coating for ivory boards, slate alloy, graphite supports and restrained red trim. Snow, evergreen foliage and bark remain opaque and non-emissive. Only the rink glass is transparent, at opacity 0.28 with depth writing disabled and a single transparent pass. No physical transmission or screen-space reflection pass is used. All material roles protect their non-emissive setting from global glow changes.

Image sources are shared through independently owned texture handles. The loading gate covers both new ice maps and shared Stadium coatings. Missing or stalled requests settle to neutral one-pixel fallbacks within eight seconds; late callbacks cannot resize terminal fallback textures. Tests cover reuse, surviving scenes, early disposal and stale downloads.

The static shader sky uses broad alpine overcast and a cool pale horizon. The preset's original hue 200 and brightness 15 are retained; brightness 15 normalizes to exposure 1, with controls bounded from 0 to 20. The sky needs no image download, animation or postprocessing. A warm-white key and two unshadowed cool fill lights keep actual V3 armor readable. The existing directional key remains the sole shadow light, fitting the current rectangular half-extents without changing user light intensity. A static reflection probe bakes once at 256 px on High or 128 px on Balanced/Low. Scratch geometry, owned probe targets and explicitly marked skies are released without touching another map's resources.
